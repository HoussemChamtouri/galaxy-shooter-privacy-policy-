Privacy Policy

App Name: Galaxy Shooter
Developer: Hexsoft
Last Updated: August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Galaxy Shooter mobile application developed by Hexsoft as an ad-supported free-to-play service. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs users regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information for anyone choosing to play Galaxy Shooter.

1. Information Collection and Third-Party Services

Galaxy Shooter does not require direct user account creation, nor do we collect personally identifiable information such as your real name, address, or phone number. However, the app utilizes third-party software development kits (SDKs) and advertising networks that may collect device identifiers and usage statistics to provide relevant advertisements and gameplay functionality.

Third-party service providers utilized by the app may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
  • AppLovin

2. Advertising and Device Identifiers

To support the free availability of the game, advertisements are displayed within the game. Ad networks may collect and process anonymous device data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) / Android ID.
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
  • Coarse location data (country or region-level).
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, impressions).

3. Log Data and Error Diagnostics

Whenever you experience a technical error or unexpected crash while playing Galaxy Shooter, automated diagnostic data (via third-party crash reporting tools) may be recorded. This includes diagnostic information, operating system state, and timestamp logs to help us identify and resolve technical issues.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Galaxy Shooter is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If a parent or guardian discovers that their child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly take steps to delete such data from our records.

5. Data Security & Retention

We value your trust and implement industry-standard practices to protect any anonymous data collected. However, please remember that no method of electronic transmission or mobile storage is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Data Deletion and User Rights

Users have the right to reset or limit their Advertising ID at any time via their Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete advertising ID). If you have questions regarding data collected through third-party services, you may reach out to us at the contact address provided below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will become effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
